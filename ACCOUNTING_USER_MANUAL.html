1 Core System Architecture & Financial Data Flow

FLUMENX OS operates on a strict, synchronized double-entry accounting engine. Money entering or leaving the agency is tracked through dedicated sub-ledgers and automatically posted to the general ledger.

Three Inviolable Principles Enforced by the System
  1. Zero Discrepancy Invariant: No journal voucher or transaction can be posted unless Total Debits = Total Credits down to the exact cent (difference < ₹0.01).
  2. Audit Immutability & Reversals: Posted journals cannot be edited or silently deleted. If a mistake occurs, the accountant clicks Reverse to generate an exact counter-entry with mandatory audit notes.
  3. Sub-Ledger Integration: Customer invoices and vendor bills must be created inside their respective modules (not as loose journal vouchers) to ensure Trade Debtors, Trade Creditors, and Aging schedules remain accurate.

Protected System Accounts vs. Custom Accounts
Accounts with isSystemAccount = true (like 1130 Bank, 1140 Debtors, 2110 Creditors, 2120 Payroll, 2150 GST) are locked from deletion because the billing and payroll engines post to them automatically. You can add as many custom accounts as needed (e.g., individual partner capital accounts, specific project expense codes, secondary bank accounts) by clicking "+ Add New Account".
Special Balance Rule: Contra Accounts
Accounts such as 1215 Accumulated Depreciation - Computers and 1225 Accumulated Depreciation - Furniture reside in the 1000s (Assets) but carry a natural CREDIT balance. This reduces the net carrying value of fixed assets directly on the Balance Sheet.

SOP 4: Direct Operational Expenses & Petty Cash Disbursements Quick Spend

For minor routine expenses that do not require full vendor bills (courier, tea/coffee pantry, software licenses, travel):

  1. Navigate to Expenses tab > click "+ Record Expense".
  2. Select Category: e.g. OFFICE_EXPENSE, TECH_EXPENSE, SALES_EXPENSE.
  3. Select Expense Account: e.g. 5230 Employee Welfare & Refreshments or 5420 SaaS Software Subscriptions.
  4. Select Paid From (Source Account):
    • 1120 Petty Cash — If paid using cash from the office cash box.
    • 1130 HDFC Bank — If paid using corporate debit/credit card or UPI.
  5. Enter Amount, Tax Amount (if GST receipt available), Title, and Merchant note. Click Save Expense.

Auto-posting: Dr. Relevant Expense Account (5xxx) | Cr. Paid From Account (1120 or 1130).

SOP 7: Weekly Bank Reconciliation Internal Control

At the end of every week or month, reconcile the general ledger bank balance with the official bank statement:

  1. Navigate to Banking & Treasury tab > select the bank account (HDFC Bank).
  2. Click "Reconcile Account".
  3. Enter the Statement Date and the Statement Ending Balance directly from the bank's netbanking portal/PDF statement.
  4. Review the list of unreconciled transactions. Check off each transaction that appears on the bank statement.
  5. Check the real-time Discrepancy counter. When it reaches ₹ 0.00, click "Finalize Reconciliation".
SOP 8: Error Corrections & Audited Reversals Audit Trail

FLUMENX OS strictly forbids silent manual deletion of posted vouchers to prevent accounting fraud. If a voucher was posted with incorrect accounts or amounts:

  1. Go to Journal Vouchers tab and locate the erroneous voucher.
  2. Click the Reverse (Undo arrow) icon.
  3. Enter the mandatory Reversal Reason (e.g. "Duplicate bank charge entry - Ref UTR-9021").
  4. Confirm. The engine posts an exact counter-journal voucher, updates account balances, and logs the action into the permanent audit trail.

5 Statutory Financial Reports & Closing Checklists

Reports in the Financial Reports tab update in real time from posted journals without requiring manual month-end closing routines:

Report What It Calculates Accounting Proof / Inspection Check
1. Balance Sheet Cumulative Assets, Liabilities, and Equity as of any selected date. Verifies fundamental invariant: Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Automatically computes live Net Working Capital (Current Assets - Current Liabilities).
2. Profit & Loss (P&L) Operational revenue vs direct costs and operating expenses over any period. Calculates Gross Margin, Operating Profit, and Net Profit. Profit automatically flows into Current Year Net P&L (3030) in Equity.
3. Trial Balance Master listing of all active account codes with opening, period debits/credits, and closing balances. Mathematical proof of double-entry integrity. Total Debit column must equal Total Credit column exactly.
4. AR & AP Aging Breakdown of unpaid invoices and supplier bills into 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 90+ day overdue buckets. Controls debtor collection timelines and avoids vendor supply holds.
Monthly Accounting Closing Checklist
  1. Verify Trial Balance: Ensure Total Dr = Total Cr and inspect any unexpected debit/credit balances.
  2. Finalize Bank Reconciliation: Reconcile all bank and cash drawers to zero discrepancy.
  3. Post Month-End Adjustments: Accrue monthly employee salaries, office rent, and fixed asset depreciation.
  4. Reconcile GST Accounts: Offset 2155 GST Input Credit (ITC) against 2150 GST Output Payable before filing GSTR-3B.
  5. Export Audit CSVs: Download monthly General Ledger and Trial Balance CSVs for company records and tax filing.
Accountant Golden Rules
  1. Never post client invoices or vendor bills via manual journals — always use Sales & Invoicing and Purchases & Bills to ensure sub-ledgers, GST registers, and aging reports stay synchronized.
  2. Always include external reference numbers (UTR, Cheque #, Vendor Bill #) and comprehensive narrations for audit trails.
  3. Perform weekly bank reconciliations every Friday in the Banking & Treasury tab to match netbanking statement closing balances with the general ledger.
